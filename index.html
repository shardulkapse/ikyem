The reinforcement you can't see.

Our internal plastic cup system is the key construction feature of a Seamcraft ball. It does two jobs at once.

It helps the ball stay round

When a ball is struck, the internal structure supports the core and helps the ball recover its spherical form. That reduces how noticeably it deforms over an innings.

A ball that goes out of round changes how it comes off the pitch and how it sits in the hand. Holding shape is holding performance.

It puts a barrier in front of the cork

Cricket is played on dewy evenings and damp grounds. The moulded cups form an additional barrier around the internal core, helping reduce how much moisture penetrates toward the cork.

That matters because moisture reaching the core is what shifts a ball's weight, hardness, bounce and feel mid-match.

Many balls rely on thin leather patches for internal reinforcement. A moulded cup system is a structural component in its own right, which is why it behaves differently after forty overs, not just out of the box.

Leather and cork, chosen properly.

Alum-tanned leather

Leather responds to its environment. Poorly processed or inadequately tanned leather takes on and gives up moisture more readily, and that shows up as changes in feel, hardness, weight and shape.

A properly executed alum-tanning process helps the leather hold more consistent physical characteristics as temperature and humidity move around it.

  • Carefully selected hides
  • Better resistance to moisture fluctuation
  • Consistent feel from ball to ball
  • Improved durability through repeated use

Quality cork core

The cork core is the single component that most defines a ball's character. It carries the bounce, the hardness, the impact response and the weight distribution.

We source cork from a supplier that also supplies established cricket-ball manufacturers, because consistency in the cork is what makes one Seamcraft ball behave like the next.

  • Consistent bounce and hardness
  • Predictable weight distribution
  • Supports shape retention
  • Ball-to-ball consistency

Paint that holds on

A ball's coating isn't cosmetic. On lower-quality balls the surface visibly chips away after relatively limited use, and the ball looks spent long before it is.

Seamcraft uses a durable paint system chosen for strong adhesion and resistance to chipping and flaking through normal use. Play will wear any leather ball down in the end — what matters is that it wears rather than peels.
